Kip and voice typing

Kip's AI actions (Polish, Tone, Translate, Reply and Chat) and voice typing can be tried free for 7 days or 100 uses, whichever comes first. If you have used them fewer than 15 times by then, the trial carries on until you have. The trial is counted on your phone, with no account.

After the trial you can keep using them with either of two purchases. Both give the same access. Neither adds features that the trial does not have.

  • One-time unlock: a single payment that unlocks Kip and voice typing with no limit.
  • Subscription: a payment that renews every 3 months until you cancel it.

Payments, renewals and refunds

Google Play takes the payment and manages the purchase. We never see your card or payment details. The price shown in the app before you buy is the price you pay, in your local currency and including any taxes Google Play adds.

A subscription renews automatically at the end of each 3 month period until you cancel it. To cancel, open Google Play, go to Payments and subscriptions, then Subscriptions, and choose Grafilo. You keep access until the end of the period you have paid for. Cancelling does not refund the current period.

Refunds follow Google Play's refund policy. If a subscription ends, or a purchase is refunded, Kip and voice typing lock again once the trial is used up. The rest of the keyboard keeps working.

To use a purchase on another phone, sign in to the same Google account, open Grafilo, choose "Free trial and unlock" and tap "Restore purchase".
