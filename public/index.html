Your writing friend, on your phone.

Grafilo is a keyboard with a built-in AI helper called Kip. It fixes your grammar, rewrites, translates and suggests replies, and it all runs on your phone. Your words never leave it.

Get Grafilo How it stays private

Android only. Works without an internet connection after a one-time download.

Ask Kip for a hand

Tap Kip above the keys when you want help. Otherwise it is a fast, ordinary keyboard.

Fix and polish

Corrects grammar and spelling and keeps your voice. Pick from a few versions.

Change the tone

Make a message friendlier, more formal, shorter or more confident.

Translate

Write in your language, send in theirs, without switching apps.

Reply for you

Kip reads the message on screen and drafts replies. Add a short note to steer it.

Talk instead of type

Voice typing that is cleaned up for you, with the speech model on your phone.

Ask anything

A private chat with Kip. Nothing is saved when you close it.

Private because of where it runs

Most AI keyboards send what you type to a server. Grafilo does the work on your phone, so there is nothing to send.

Get started in a minute

The first setup downloads Kip. It is a large file, so Wi-Fi is best.

  1. Install Grafilo from Google Play.
  2. Turn it on in Android's keyboard settings and choose it as your keyboard.
  3. Download Kip. You can leave the app while it downloads.
  4. Start typing. Tap Kip whenever you want help.
