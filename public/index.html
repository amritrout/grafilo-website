Everything you do with words, in one keyboard

Tap the AI bar above the keys when you want help. The rest of the time it is a fast, ordinary keyboard.

Polish

Fix grammar and spelling, rephrase a sentence, or add emojis. Select part of your text to change only that part.

Tone

Make it professional, casual, friendly, formal, confident, empathetic, sarcastic or poetic. Add tones of your own.

Translate

Write in your language and send it in another, without leaving the chat.

Reply

Grafilo reads the message on your screen and drafts answers. Type a few words, like “say yes after 2”, and it writes the message for you.

Voice typing

Talk the way you normally do. The ums and false starts are cleaned up, and you can say “make it friendlier” to change the result.

Chat

Ask Kip anything, like how to say no politely. The chat is forgotten when you close it.

Meet Kip

Kip is a small keycap robot who lives in your keyboard and does the helping. It thinks while it works, cheers when it is done, and wakes up when you open the keyboard after a while.

You can choose how much it moves, or turn the animations off.

Private because of where it runs

Most AI keyboards send what you type to a server. Grafilo does the work on your phone, so there is nothing to send.

On your phoneKip's language model and the speech model are stored on your device.
No accountNo sign-up, and no cloud subscription for the AI.
Works offlineThe internet is only used to download the models once.
No ads, no trackingNo advertising or analytics in the keyboard.

Reply reads the screen only when you tap it, and you turn that on yourself. Read the privacy policy for exactly what the app uses and why.

Get started in a minute

Setup downloads Kip. It is a large file, so Wi-Fi is best. You can leave the app while it downloads.

  1. Install Grafilo from Google Play.
  2. Turn it on in Android's keyboard settings and choose it as your keyboard.
  3. Pick your languages from 12, for typing and for speaking.
  4. Download Kip. Then tap the AI bar whenever you want help.
